How SwingKrazy Works

Upload your golf swing videos and get instant AI-powered feedback. No cloud, no uploads — everything stays on your computer.

1

Understanding Golf Swing Phases

Even if you don't play golf, SwingKrazy breaks down any swing into four key moments. Think of it like analyzing a dance move frame-by-frame.

🏌️ Address
The starting position. The golfer stands still, facing the target, ready to begin the swing. This is the baseline — we measure everything relative to this moment.

Similar in other sports:
⚾ Baseball: The batter's stance before the pitch arrives
🎾 Tennis: Ready position, feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent
🥊 Pickleball: The set position before returning serve
📈 Top of Backswing
The golfer has wound up like a spring, bringing the club up and back. At this moment, the upper body is fully rotated away from the target. Maximum "coil" happens here.

Similar in other sports:
⚾ Baseball: The moment before the bat starts forward (hands loaded, bat cocked back)
🎾 Tennis: Backswing at ear height, shoulders coiled, ready to drive forward
🥊 Pickleball: Paddle drawn back, shoulder rotated, coiled for the shot
⚡ Impact
The moment the club touches the ball. This is where power transfers to the ball. The golfer's position at impact determines the shot's direction and quality.

Similar in other sports:
⚾ Baseball: The instant the bat meets the ball (contact point)
🎾 Tennis: Racket face meets ball at the optimal height and angle
🥊 Pickleball: Paddle contact with the ball (determines spin and direction)
🎯 Finish
The follow-through position after impact. The swing is complete. A balanced finish indicates good control and energy transfer.

Similar in other sports:
⚾ Baseball: Follow-through after contact (hands wrap around body, front leg stable)
🎾 Tennis: Follow-through across body, weight transferred to front foot
🥊 Pickleball: Follow-through with arm extended, body balanced and ready for next shot

What Gets Measured

SwingKrazy uses AI pose estimation to track 33 body landmarks (joints) and calculate precise angles.

Metric What It Measures Why It Matters
Hip Angle Rotation of the hips (pelvis) Power generation starts from the ground up. Hip rotation drives the swing.
Shoulder Angle Rotation of the shoulders Shoulders follow hips. Good sequence = controlled, powerful swing.
X-Factor Difference between shoulder and hip rotation The "coil" — creates torque. Bigger difference = more potential power.
Spine Tilt Angle of the spine from vertical Affects balance and how the club approaches the ball.
Wrist Hinge Angle at the wrist (club angle) Controls club face angle and impact position.
💡 Compare to a Pro
SwingKrazy compares your angles at each phase to a professional golfer's swing. See exactly where your mechanics differ.

Access Sample Videos (Optional)

Want to test SwingKrazy before uploading your own videos? We provide sample professional and amateur swings to explore. Here's how to access them.

📥 Getting Sample Videos — Clone the Repository

Choose one of these methods to get the sample videos:

Method 1: Command Line (Easiest)

Use your terminal (Mac/Linux) or Command Prompt (Windows).

  1. Open Terminal/Command Prompt
  2. Navigate to where you want to save the project:
    cd ~/Documents
  3. Run the clone command:
    git clone https://github.com/KnowingPath-ai/swingkrazy.git
  4. Wait for download to complete (includes sample videos)
  5. Your videos are in: swingkrazy/raw-videos/CV-golf-analysis/

Method 2: GitHub Desktop (Most User-Friendly)

If you prefer a visual interface, use GitHub Desktop app.

  1. Download GitHub Desktop
  2. Sign in with your GitHub account
  3. Click "File" → "Clone Repository"
  4. Paste: https://github.com/KnowingPath-ai/swingkrazy.git
  5. Choose where to save (e.g., Documents folder)
  6. Click "Clone"

Method 3: Download as ZIP (No Git Required)

If you don't have Git or GitHub Desktop installed.

  1. Visit github.com/KnowingPath-ai/swingkrazy
  2. Click the green "Code" button (top right)
  3. Click "Download ZIP"
  4. Extract the ZIP file to your desired location
  5. Sample videos are in: swingkrazy-main/raw-videos/CV-golf-analysis/
✅ All Methods Work
All three methods get you the same files. Choose whichever is easiest for you. Command Line is fastest if you're comfortable with it; GitHub Desktop is most visual; ZIP download requires no software installation.

Upload Your Videos

SwingKrazy requires two videos: your swing and a professional golfer's swing for comparison. Use sample videos from the repo or upload your own.

🎥 Your Swing

What to film:

  • Film from the side (90° angle to the target line)
  • Entire body visible in frame
  • Landscape orientation recommended
  • Steady camera (use tripod if possible)
  • Good lighting
  • 1–3 full swings in one video

File formats: MP4, MOV, or WebM. Recommended: MP4 for best browser compatibility.

⭐ Professional Golfer's Swing

Where to get it:

  • YouTube golf instruction videos
  • PGA Tour highlight videos
  • Golf instruction apps (Swing Profile, etc.)
  • Personal coaching video (if available)

Same requirements:

  • Side-on camera angle
  • Entire body visible
  • Similar height/build (or SwingKrazy will normalize)

⭐ Strongly Suggested:

Choose a professional golfer with a similar body build, height, and weight to yourself. Or pick your favorite golfer — analyzing against someone you admire is more motivating!

①

Open SwingKrazy

Open the app in your web browser. You'll see two upload zones: one for "Your Swing" and one for "Pro Golfer's Swing".

②

Upload Your Swing Video

Click the "Your Swing" upload area. Select your MP4 or MOV file (must be landscape or portrait, side-on view). The app will load the video.

③

Upload Pro Reference Video

Click the "Pro Golfer's Swing" upload area. Select a professional golfer's swing video in the same format.

④

Click "Analyze"

Once both videos are loaded, the "Analyze" button will appear. Click it. The app will process both videos frame-by-frame (this takes 30–60 seconds depending on video length).

⑤

Review Your Results

See your swing broken down by phase, with metrics, skeleton overlays, and coaching cues. Compare yourself to the pro side-by-side.

5

Your Results Dashboard

After analysis, SwingKrazy displays your swing in multiple ways:

📊 Phase Metrics Table
Shows your angle measurements at each phase (Address, Top, Impact, Finish) compared to the pro's angles. Green = good, yellow = needs work.
🦴 Skeleton Overlay
Your body is drawn as a stick figure on a canvas. The pro's skeleton is overlaid in a different color so you can see side-by-side how your positions differ at each phase.
🔥 Heatmap
Shows which joints moved the most during your swing. Red = maximum movement, blue = minimal movement. Reveals where energy is concentrated.
📈 Tempo Chart
Displays the ratio of backswing speed to downswing speed. Pro swings typically have a faster downswing than backswing.
⚖️ Weight Shift
Tracks how your body's center of gravity moves through the swing. Shows if weight transfers correctly to generate power.
🎯 Coaching Cues
AI-generated tips based on your metrics. Example: "Your hip rotation at impact is 15° behind the pro. Try rotating more on the downswing."
🔒 Privacy First
All analysis happens on your computer. Videos are never uploaded to the cloud. Your swing data stays private and never leaves your device.

Tips for Best Results

📐 Camera Angle
Shoot from the side (90°), not from behind or in front. The AI needs to see hip and shoulder rotation clearly. For fairness, film your pro reference video from the same angle.
🌞 Lighting
Bright, even lighting helps the AI detect joints accurately. Avoid backlighting (sun behind the golfer). If indoors, use consistent overhead or side lighting.
📱 Video Length
1–3 swings per video is ideal. Shorter videos process faster. Very long videos (5+ minutes) may take longer to analyze.
👥 Body Visibility
The entire body must be visible (head to feet). Don't crop the frame. The AI tracks all joints, from head position to ankle angles.
📏 Similar Context
For fair comparison, choose a pro golfer of similar height and build. SwingKrazy normalizes for body size, but matching physical characteristics gives better insights.
🎬 File Format
Use MP4 for maximum browser compatibility. MOV files (iPhone) work but may require conversion. WebM also works if already in that format.
7

Troubleshooting

Video won't load
Try: Convert to MP4 format (use ffmpeg or a video converter). Check that the file is not corrupted. Ensure your browser is up-to-date (Edge, Chrome, Firefox).
Analyze button is disabled
Why: Both videos must be loaded. Upload both "Your Swing" and "Pro Golfer's Swing" before clicking Analyze.
Skeleton looks wrong or distorted
Why: Poor lighting, motion blur, or the golfer is not fully visible in frame. Try filming in brighter conditions or from a steadier position. Ensure entire body is visible.
Analysis takes too long
Why: Long videos with many swings take longer to analyze. Try uploading a shorter clip. Processing time is proportional to video length.
Metrics don't seem right
Likely cause: Camera angle is not side-on, or the golfer is not fully visible. Re-film from a pure 90° side angle with the entire body in frame.
